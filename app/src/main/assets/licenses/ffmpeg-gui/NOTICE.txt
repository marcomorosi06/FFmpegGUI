CORRESPONDING SOURCE
The complete source code of this APK is:
- the FFmpeg GUI repository, https://github.com/marcomorosi06/FFmpegGUI ,
  at the tag of this release;
- FFmpeg, FFmpegKitNext and the libraries built into the FFmpeg runtime,
  at the commits pinned in scripts/ffmpeg-kit-next.lock of that tag, built
  by scripts/build-ffmpeg-kit-next.sh;
- the video effects (assets/effects/): their source is
  scripts/generate-effects.py of that tag, which draws every frame and
  encodes them with FFmpeg (python3 scripts/generate-effects.py), with
  scripts/punch3d.py, which renders the 3D fist of the punch transitions
  and makes the sound of its blow (punch.mp4, punch_back.mp4 and their
  WebP frame sheets: pictures and sounds alike), and
  scripts/math_effect.py, which draws the formulas of the math effect
  with its own stroke font (math.mp4, math.jpg), and scripts/tv_break.py,
  which draws the TV break's test card and channel changes and composes
  and plays its tune (tv_intro.mp4, tv_card.mp4, tv_outro.mp4 and their
  frame sheets: pictures and sounds alike);
- sherpa-onnx v1.13.8 (https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx) and the
  sources its build pins, and ONNX Runtime 1.28.0
  (https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime).

CODE BASED ON OTHER PROJECTS
- The splash screen (res/drawable/splash_icon.xml, res/drawable-v31/
  splash_icon_animated*.xml, the Splash themes in res/values-v31/themes.xml
  and its exit in MainActivity: the animated shapes, their timing and the
  hand-over to the app) comes from WiFi Audio Streaming for Android,
  https://github.com/marcomorosi06/WiFiAudioStreaming-Android , by the same author
  (Copyright (c) 2025-2026 Marco Morosi and contributors), under the same
  licence: EUPL-1.2, its text as that repository has it in
  ../wifi-audio-streaming/LICENSE.txt. The glyph in it, its scale and the
  colours are FFmpeg GUI's own.
- The audio editor's spectrum (core/audio/Spectrum.kt: the window, the FFT,
  the bands, the tilt, the decibel range, the rise and fall of the bars and
  their caps; AudioSpectrum.kt draws it in the same spirit) comes from WiFi
  Audio Streaming's desktop app (SpectrumAnalyzer.kt and SpectrumView.kt),
  https://github.com/marcomorosi06/WiFiAudioStreaming-Desktop , by the same
  author (Copyright (c) 2026 Marco Morosi), under the same licence:
  EUPL-1.2, the same text as ../wifi-audio-streaming/LICENSE.txt. Several
  sounds added as their powers, the timing in seconds and the sound read as
  it is heard (preview/SpectrumTap.kt) are FFmpeg GUI's own.
- The stem separation (core/stems) computes the spectrograms and masks of
  Bandit v2 (torchaudio's transform as the authors' configuration sets it;
  code Apache-2.0, weights CC-BY-SA-4.0, ../bandit/) and of Spleeter's five
  stems (as Spleeter computes them; MIT, ../spleeter/LICENSE.txt), written
  in Kotlin; the models are downloaded by the user. Its overlap-add across
  windows started from the way sherpa-onnx (Apache-2.0, ../sherpa-onnx/
  LICENSE.txt) runs Spleeter's two stems, which the app used before.
- The live preview of the transitions between clips (core/preview/
  TransitionLook.kt, and the recipes of the transitions FFmpeg's xfade has,
  core/transition/Recipes.kt) works out the math of FFmpeg's xfade filter
  (libavfilter/vf_xfade.c, LGPL-2.1-or-later, ../ffmpeg/LICENSE.md), written
  again in Kotlin and AGSL; the other transitions are this project's own.
- The text-art engine (core/textart) is this project's own code; it follows
  published definitions: OKLab's matrices as Bjorn Ottosson published them
  (https://bottosson.github.io/posts/oklab/, code in the public domain),
  CIEDE2000 as Sharma, Wu and Dalal formulated it (2005), Bayer's ordered
  dithering and the Floyd-Steinberg and Atkinson error diffusion, Paul
  Bourke's seventy-step character ramp, xterm's 256-colour palette and the
  terminal themes' colour values, IBM code page 437's character table and
  the SAUCE record as the ANSI art scene specified it.
- The dubbing runs Kokoro-82M (hexgrad, Apache-2.0, ../kokoro/LICENSE.txt)
  through sherpa-onnx's own text-to-speech, already in the APK; the model
  and its eSpeak NG data are downloaded by the user.

MODIFIED THIRD-PARTY FILES
sherpa-onnx v1.13.8 (Apache-2.0, ../sherpa-onnx/LICENSE.txt), whose
libsherpa-onnx-jni.so also contains eSpeak NG (GPL-3.0-or-later,
../sherpa-onnx/espeak-ng/), is not packed as its release AAR
(sherpa-onnx-1.13.8.aar, pinned by its SHA-256) was published. The app
packs a copy of that AAR, made by app/build.gradle.kts when the project is
built, changed by Marco Morosi in 2026 as follows:
- modified: lib/<abi>/libsherpa-onnx-jni.so, the only change being the
  version indexes (in its .gnu.version section) of the symbols it takes
  from libonnxruntime.so, set to "global", so that it runs on the ONNX
  Runtime 1.28.0 the app ships (two builds of libonnxruntime.so cannot be
  loaded in one process); no code and no other byte is changed;
- removed: lib/<abi>/libonnxruntime.so (ONNX Runtime 1.28.2, replaced by
  ONNX Runtime 1.28.0 from Maven Central), lib/<abi>/libsherpa-onnx-c-api.so
  and lib/<abi>/libsherpa-onnx-cxx-api.so (not used by the app).
The unmodified sources of these files are those of sherpa-onnx v1.13.8 and
of the sources its build pins (see CORRESPONDING SOURCE); the program that
makes the change is unversionImportsFrom in app/build.gradle.kts.
