CORRESPONDING SOURCE
The complete source code of this APK is:
- the FFmpeg GUI repository, https://github.com/marcomorosi06/FFmpegGUI ,
  at the tag of this release;
- FFmpeg, FFmpegKitNext and the libraries built into the FFmpeg runtime,
  at the commits pinned in scripts/ffmpeg-kit-next.lock of that tag, built
  by scripts/build-ffmpeg-kit-next.sh;
- the video effects (assets/effects/): their source is
  scripts/generate-effects.py of that tag, which draws every frame and
  encodes them with FFmpeg (python3 scripts/generate-effects.py);
- sherpa-onnx v1.13.8 (https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx) and the
  sources its build pins, and ONNX Runtime 1.28.0
  (https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime).

CODE BASED ON OTHER PROJECTS
- The voice separation (core/voice) computes Spleeter's spectrogram, masks
  and overlap-add the way sherpa-onnx does (Apache-2.0, ../sherpa-onnx/
  LICENSE.txt), written again in Kotlin; the model is Spleeter's, by
  Deezer (MIT, ../spleeter/LICENSE.txt), downloaded by the user.
- The dubbing runs Kokoro-82M (hexgrad, Apache-2.0, ../kokoro/LICENSE.txt)
  through sherpa-onnx's own text-to-speech, already in the APK; the model
  and its eSpeak NG data are downloaded by the user.
